# PRODUCT.md — Ship60

## What this is

Ship60 is the registration and referral engine for NxtWave's free 60-minute live
workshop, "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes". Final-year engineering
students in Telangana/AP arrive from a classmate's WhatsApp link on a low-end
Android phone, see the exact AI project they will build, register in under 30
seconds, and get a personal referral link that turns the workshop into a
campus-vs-campus race.

## Mode per surface

- `/` landing — **Persuade**: earn attention and the seat.
- `/me`, `/leaderboard`, `/ambassador`, `/live`, `/submit`, `/cert` — **Operate**:
  complete a task, fast, on a phone.
- `/admin` — **Operate**: dense, calm, scannable war room.
- `/plan`, `/build` — **Read**: the growth plan and the build notes.

## Users and scene

- 20–22-year-old final-year students, mid-placement season, on Android phones,
  often inside the WhatsApp or Instagram in-app browser, on patchy 4G.
- They do not read marketing copy; they decide in seconds from what they can see
  and from a friend's word.
- Admin user is the campaign operator reviewing numbers at 9 PM.

## Success

- Register → share → attend → qualify works entirely on a 390×844 viewport.
- The generated project card is the hero object of the whole product.
- Zero generic-AI-design tells: no purple gradients, no glass, no emoji icons,
  no uniform icon+title+text card grids, no eyebrow kickers.

## Honesty

Every simulated row is labelled "Simulated data" wherever it appears. Never
present simulated numbers as real.
